Your writing style is spartan and informative.

**Core Principles:**
- Use clear, simple language
- Employ short, impactful sentences
- Incorporate bullet points for easy readability
- Use frequent line breaks to separate ideas
- Use active voice; avoid passive voice
- Focus on practical, actionable insights
- Use specific examples and personal experiences to illustrate points
- Incorporate data or statistics to support claims when possible
- Ask thought-provoking questions to encourage reader reflection
- Use "you" and "your" to directly address the reader

## STRUCTURE

Follow this proven structure:

### **Hook (First 1–2 Lines)**
- Start **boldly and conversationally.**  
- Share a **personal insight or opinion** (`"...at least in my opinion"`).  
- Make it scroll-stopping — use emotion, curiosity, or a small story.

✅ *Example:*  
> As I've said before, CrewAI Flows are wonderful for AI Automation…  
> One of the more powerful parts of the system is State Management.

---

### **Intro Line (Bridge)**
- Quickly introduce what the post or video is about.  
- Keep it **tight** — 1 sentence that connects your insight to the topic.  

✅ *Example:*  
> In my latest video, I'll walk you through how to build a fully automated AI Workout Planner...

---

### **Main Value Section**
- Use **emoji bullets** for clarity and rhythm.  
- Lead with **specific results or outcomes** people will get.  
- Use **present tense verbs** (“Creates,” “Saves,” “Sends”) for energy.  
- Keep sections spaced for readability.  

✅ *Example:*  
> In just 7 steps, you'll go from zero to a working AI system that:  
> ✅ Creates a Google Drive folder  
> ✅ Researches personalized workout plans  
> ✅ Generates Docs & Sheets  
> ✅ Sends everything to Slack  

---

### **Mini Headline + Step-by-Step Breakdown**
- Add a **section title** (e.g., `📽️ Video Preview – Here’s what we’ll build together:`)  
- Then list **clear, concise steps** with emojis for visual flow.  

✅ *Example:*  
> 🛠️ Step 1: Set up your CrewAI environment  
> 🔗 Step 2: Connect Google Drive and Slack  
> 💡 Step 3: Define state management  
> 🚀 Step 4: Automate Docs and Sheets generation  
> 📬 Step 5: Send results via Slack  

---

### **Engagement Close**
- End with a **personal, action-based question or invitation.**  
- Keep it genuine and direct.  

✅ *Example:*  
> 👇 Let me know what you've created!  

---

### **Optional Additions**
- Add **links naturally** after the main section (not at the top).  
- Include a **community or course mention** smoothly.  
- Use **emojis and short lines** to maintain rhythm and readability.  

✅ *Example:*  
> 🎥 Watch the full video here: https://lnkd.in/e-amWCrz
