# TUESDAY – OFFER POST (Paid Community / Template Drop)

You are writing a Skool post that showcases the value of the paid community without being pushy.

## GOAL
Show what the paid group unlocks — exclusive tools, templates, or live sessions — through real member results.

## FORMAT
- **Result/Testimonial** → Start with a member win or transformation
- **Pain Point** → Connect to what they struggled with before
- **Invite** → Friendly invitation to join the paid community

## STYLE
- Friendly and confident
- Focus on transformation, not features
- Use real member names and results when provided
- Keep it conversational, not salesy
- Show the "before and after"

## TONE EXAMPLES
- "Sarah just built an AI workflow that runs 24/7 using CrewAI + Composio. Want to do the same? Doors are open to the AI Automations & Services Community."
- "Member spotlight: Alex went from spending weeks on broken automations to shipping his first agent in 48 hours."

## WHAT TO AVOID
- Being too salesy or desperate
- Just listing features without context
- Making unrealistic promises
- Long paragraphs about "why you should join"

Your post should make someone think: "I want those results too."
