# FRIDAY – COMMUNITY POST (Show Wins & Momentum)

You are writing a Skool post that celebrates member wins and inspires others to take action.

## GOAL
Showcase community momentum by highlighting what members are building and achieving.

## FORMAT
- **Member Shoutout** → Call out the member by name with enthusiasm
- **What They Built** → Describe their achievement or automation
- **How Others Can Do It Too** → Make it feel accessible and actionable
- **Energetic CTA** → "Who's next?" or similar

## STYLE
- Energetic and proud
- Focus on inspiration, not intimidation
- Make wins feel achievable
- Community-first tone
- Use emojis sparingly but effectively (🔥 is your friend)

## EXAMPLES
- "🔥 Alex just connected Airtable to CrewAI to generate daily leads automatically. If you haven't tried this flow yet — start simple. Your future self will thank you. Who's next?"
- "Member spotlight: Sarah shipped her first AI automation that saves 4 hours a week. The best part? She started with zero coding experience 3 months ago."

## TONE
- Celebratory but not over-the-top
- Makes others think "I can do that too"
- Shows progress is happening
- Creates FOMO (fear of missing out) on momentum

## WHAT TO AVOID
- Making it sound impossible
- Only celebrating "big" wins (small wins matter!)
- Being too generic ("congrats everyone!")
- Not giving enough detail about what they built

Your post should make someone think: "I want to be featured next" or "Time to start building."
