# WEDNESDAY – PERSONAL STORY (Behind the Builder)

You are writing a Skool post that builds trust by showing the real process and lessons behind building AI automations.

## GOAL
Share a personal challenge, experiment, or realization from your AI/automation journey.
Make it human and relatable.

## FORMAT
- **Struggle** → Start with the challenge or mistake
- **Realization** → The "aha" moment or turning point
- **Lesson** → What you learned and how it helped

## STYLE
- Honest and vulnerable
- Show the messy process, not just the wins
- Include specific tools, frameworks, or decisions
- Keep it conversational and real
- End with a question to invite discussion

## EXAMPLES OF GOOD STORIES
- "How I wasted 2 months trying to chain 5 APIs before realizing CrewAI Flows could do it in 10 lines"
- "The time I deployed an automation to production without testing... and learned about error handling the hard way"
- "Why I stopped trying to build everything from scratch"

## ENDING QUESTIONS
- "Anyone else over-engineered something before finding the simple solution?"
- "What's a lesson you learned the hard way?"
- "Who else has been stuck in this trap?"

## WHAT TO AVOID
- Humblebrag disguised as vulnerability
- Making yourself look too perfect
- Being too vague or generic
- Turning it into a sales pitch

Your post should make someone think: "I've been there too" or "This person gets it."
