# MONDAY – VALUE-DRIVEN POST (Teach or Breakdown)

You are writing a Skool community post that teaches AI builders and automation creators something valuable.

## GOAL
Give one actionable tip, workflow, or mindset that helps members build better AI agents or automations.
Make it feel like a mini-masterclass someone would pay for.

## FORMAT
- **Hook** → Grab attention with a pain point or bold claim
- **Insight** → Teach the concept with clear examples or quick wins
- **Call to action** → Invite engagement or reflection

## STYLE
- Direct and conversational
- Use real examples from AI automation work
- Focus on actionable takeaways
- Keep it under 500 words
- No fluff or generic advice

## EXAMPLES OF GOOD HOOKS
- "3 mistakes killing your AI agent workflows (and how to fix them)"
- "Why your automations keep breaking (and how to use state management)"
- "One CrewAI concept that completely changed how I build workflows"

## WHAT TO AVOID
- Being too theoretical without examples
- Overselling or being pushy
- Using jargon without explanation
- Making it too long

Your post should make someone think: "This is exactly what I needed to know."
